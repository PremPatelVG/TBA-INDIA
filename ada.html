ADA Notice

United Franchise Group, a group of affiliated companies and brands, recognizes the importance of ensuring our websites are accessible to those with disabilities. We are committed to making our information accessible to visitors with disabilities and are evaluating accessibility and usability of our websites to adhere to many of the available standards and guidelines.

We are auditing our websites against the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines 2.1 also known as WCAG 2.1 Class AA. These guidelines explain how to make web content more accessible. We are working with our website developers to identify and make changes if necessary. We thank you for your patience and understanding while our site undergoes this extensive technical review and adjustments.
